Cookie Policy

Information on the cookies used by the euriskosrl.it site, in line with EU Regulation 2016/679 and the Italian Data Protection Authority guidelines of June 10, 2021.

Last updated: May 1, 2026 · Version 1.0

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that the websites you visit send to your device, where they are stored to be sent back to the same sites on subsequent visits. They are used for IT authentication, session monitoring and to store information about user activity.

Types of cookies used

Technical cookies (always active)

These cookies are necessary for the proper functioning of the site and do not require user consent.

Cookie namePurposeDurationType
eurisko_consent Stores the user's choices regarding cookies 12 months First-party

Analytics cookies — Google Analytics 4 (optional, subject to consent)

The site uses Google Analytics 4 for anonymous traffic measurement. These cookies are installed only after the user grants explicit consent via the cookie banner.

Cookie nameProviderPurposeDurationType
_ga Google LLC Anonymous traffic measurement 2 years Third-party
_ga_M936PDKB61 Google LLC Anonymous traffic measurement 2 years Third-party

Data transfer: USA — under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Note: the site implements Google Consent Mode v2: without consent no analytics cookies are installed and only anonymous pings without identifiers are sent to Google.

Vercel Analytics + Speed Insights (always active)

The site uses Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights for aggregate visit measurement and page performance monitoring. These are first-party services that do not set cookies, do not collect personal data (no PII) and are GDPR-compliant by design; they are therefore always active and do not require consent.

Marketing cookies (optional, subject to consent)

The site does not use profiling or marketing cookies.

Third-party services

The site relies on the following third-party services that may set technical or operational cookies on the user's device:

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting provider. For details: Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Fontshare (ITC Group) — provider of the web fonts used. For details: Fontshare Privacy Policy.
  • Resend (Resend, Inc.) — service used to email the contact and job application form submissions (the CV is delivered as an attachment). For details: Resend Privacy Policy.
  • Google reCAPTCHA (Google Ireland Ltd.) — anti-spam protection active on the contact and application forms; it may set technical/functional cookies. For details: Google Privacy Policy.

How to manage your preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using the banner that appears on first visit or, later, by accessing the cookie settings via the link in the page footer.

You can also manage cookies directly in your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Disabling technical cookies may affect the proper functioning of the site.
